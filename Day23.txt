Today topic is Modem

A modem in a computer network is a device used to connect a computer (or network) to the internet by converting signals between different forms.
The word modem comes from:
Mo → Modulator 
Dem → Demodulator
A modem performs two main functions:
Modulation 
Converts digital data from a computer into analog signals 
This allows data to travel over communication lines like telephone cables 
Demodulation 
Converts incoming analog signals back into digital data 
So the computer can understand the information

Modulation allows multiple signals to share the same medium at the same time using different carrier frequencies (called FDM – Frequency Division Multiplexing). This increases network capacity.
To reduce noise and interference :
Direct digital signals are more vulnerable to noise. Modulation helps shift signals into frequency bands where transmission is more stable and less noisy.
Demodulation works in reverse—it converts the RF carrier wave received from your internet provider into digital signals your home network can use.

Basic Working of Modem
ISP sends signals. 
Modem receives the signals.
 Modem converts signals into digital data. 
Data is sent to router/computer through Ethernet.
 Devices access the internet.

A router needs a modem. 
A modem can work without a router.
