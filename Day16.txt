Today topic is subnetting with vlsm

In IP subnetting, (Fixed Lenth Subnet Mask ) FLSM and (Variable Length Subnet Mask ) VLSM are two ways of dividing a network into smaller subnets.

With FLSM, every subnet uses the same subnet mask .
 It has same number of IP and usable host address.
Ex – If we subnet network 192.168.10.1/24 into /26
The subnet will 192.168.10.0/26 ,192.168.10.64/26 ,192.168.10.128/26
and 192.168.10.192/26 .
Subnet Mask : 255.255.255.192
Every Subnet will be identical with 64 IP Address and 62 Host usable address

With VLSM ,Different subnets can use different subnet masks.
It allows larger departments uses the larger subnets and smaller departments uses the smaller subnets.
It is more efficient .
Example : Suppose that we have different departments in our university and different department needs different no. of hosts.
Engineering – 5000 hosts , Exam Department – 2000 hosts , Library – 500 hosts , Sociology – 800 hosts , Arts – 600 hosts .  
We have network 172.10.0.0/16.
Here ,we can use Subnetting with VLSM .
